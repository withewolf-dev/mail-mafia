A second cold-email format, written by Gitartha by hand on 2026-08-19 for Raphaelson & Levine. It needs **no probe and no crawl** - only a web-research read of the business - so it costs ~2 minutes instead of the probe format's ~9.

**The structure (this order is the point):**

1. **Earned credit, specific.** Three real facts pulled from research, no flattery. "You've built a pretty strong position in New York injury law - 4.8 stars across 721 reviews, a Flushing office, and Mandarin intake."
2. **The hinge.** "That made me curious." Turns a pitch into a person thinking out loud.
3. **The client's question, verbatim.** "If a Mandarin-speaking injury victim asks ChatGPT or Perplexity, 'Who is a Mandarin-speaking injury lawyer near me?' does it recommend Raphaelson & Levine?"
4. **The stakes.** "Your clients are using AI to get recommendations on {specific category}. Not being ranked in those searches means losing business."
5. **The offer.** "I run those searches for {vertical}. I can test 10 real queries, the kind {specific client type} would actually type, then show you which firms come up and where you rank."
6. **"Free. No pitch attached."** then **"Want me to run it?"**

**Why step 3 is the whole trick:** it asserts nothing, so it can never be factually wrong - but it is so specific that the reader supplies the doubt himself and cannot stop wondering. It opens the same gap the probe format opens with a verified finding, at zero probe cost. Speed vs. strength stops being a tradeoff.

**Filling the slots needs real research, not CSV columns.** The category is not the CSV `vertical` ("Law") but what the business is actually known for, read off their site - Raphaelson's headline verdicts are construction/catastrophic injury, and the Flushing office + 中文（普通话）intake on their contact page is what produced the Mandarin question. See [[email-body-format]] for the plain-text rules that still apply (no em dashes - Gitartha's own draft slipped one into the sign-off).

**Weakness to know:** if the prospect replies "it does recommend us," there is no prepared answer - but that reply is a conversation, which is the goal. The probe format ([[reflow-not-write-emails]] pipeline) remains the stronger option when a verified "it named X, not you" is worth 9 minutes.
