Gitartha wants no em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) anywhere in outreach email copy, subjects included. Use a plain hyphen with spaces instead.

**Why:** em dashes read as machine-written; the copy should read like one person typing.

**How to apply:** when writing or editing subjects/bodies, use " - ". The rule is codified in skeleton.md's non-negotiables (added 2026-08-17), so generated emails inherit it; enforce it too when hand-editing stored drafts. Related: [[villages-florida-wording]], [[cold-email-hard-rules]].
