When email copy references The Villages, FL (prospect city for the Aqua Med Spa satellite, prospect #3, domain ocalaplasticsurgery.com), Gitartha wants it written as "The Villages, Florida", state included, in both subject and body.

**Why:** bare "the villages" reads as a typo or a generic phrase to anyone who doesn't know the place; the state disambiguates it as a place name.

**How to apply:** when composing or editing copy for prospects in The Villages, append ", Florida" to the place name. Related: [[mail-mafia-project]].
